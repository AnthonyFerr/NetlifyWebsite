About me

Hi! I'm Anthony, a junior studying CS at Wentworth Institute of Technology in Boston. When I was in middle school, I started learning Python to build simple bots using Twitter and Discord APIs. This quickly expanded to automating video games, building webscrapers, and creating applets for my high school math classes. I had spent so much time reading documentation and scouring Stack-Overflow during this period that I debugged a few programs while dreaming.

When I got to college, my interest in CS exploded to every niche: network programming, big data, and particularly computer architecture. I was so intrigued by low-level programming, operating systems, and hardware that I picked up a minor in Electrical Engineering as an extra challenge. This instantly became the most difficult part of my academics, but my love for problem solving and the underlying theory of computing allows me to push through.

Outside of the classroom, I continue to learn through building projects. I enjoy solving novel problems with Bash, building quality-of-life applications for my personal use, and staying up to date with the latest technologies. Beyond the tech-sphere, I also enjoy hiking in New Hampshire, all-you-can-eat sushi, and gardening.
